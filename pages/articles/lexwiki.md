**LexWiki** (fully known as **Lexibyte Wiki**)[&sup1;](#note1c){: id="note1"} is an **open-source online encyclopedia** launched by [Lexibyte](Lexibyte) on **May 4, 2025**. Its main purpose is documenting meanings of inside jokes and random things from the Horiverse.

Initially, LexWiki was planned to be powered by MediaWiki with a custom user interface. However, it was decided instead to build a separate wiki engine, dubbed the "**LexWiki Engine**," using the **Jekyll static site generator**.

As of **June 29, 2025**, LexWiki is available under the `/wiki/` directory of [HoriWebsite](HoriWebsite)'s domain (`horibyte.is-a.dev`). Since its launch, it has seen continuous growth, with a growing number of articles.

The LexWiki Engine reached **Alpha development status** on **May 6, 2025**. It is primarily developed by Lexibyte, with other tweaks and adjustments having been done by **Clyron**.

LexWiki features a clean design that incorporates elements from **Material Design Lite (MDL)**. Key functionalities of the platform include a functional search bar (found at the top of the header) and a MDL drawer for linking resources regarding the wiki, version information and copyright strings.

#### Development Overview
---

The LexWiki project began in early April 2025, initially considering a MediaWiki platform before opting to build a custom "LexWiki Engine" using Jekyll. Development started rapidly in early May, with key pre-alpha and alpha versions quickly establishing its core functionalities. Subsequent beta releases introduced its Material Design Lite layout, robust search capabilities, and a Wikipedia-styled "Did You Know" feature, continually enhancing both its appearance and content as the project progressed through its Horibyte-to-Lexibyte brand transition.
